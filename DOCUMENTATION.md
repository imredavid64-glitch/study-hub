# Study Hub — User Documentation

## Overview
Study Hub is an all-in-one AI-powered study, productivity, and exam preparation platform designed for students and self-learners. It operates seamlessly across web browsers, desktop (Electron), and mobile platforms (Capacitor / PWA).

---

## Key Features

### 1. AI Socratic Tutor & Voice Conversation
- **Socratic Guidance:** The AI tutor helps you think through problems step-by-step rather than just giving direct answers.
- **Voice Conversation Partner:** Supports live speech input via the Web Speech API and voice synthesis playback.
- **Speech & Presentation Coach:** Analyzes pacing, estimated duration, filler words, and audience reactions.

### 2. Spaced Repetition & Flashcards
- **Spaced Review:** Implements Leitner/SuperMemo spaced repetition scheduling.
- **Mobile Swipe Controls:** On touch devices, swipe right to mark cards as **Easy / Mastered** and swipe left for **Hard / Review**.
- **Auto-Sync from Mocks:** Missed questions from AI mock tests are automatically added to flashcard review decks.

### 3. Exam Estimator & Analytics
- **Live Grade Prediction:** Blends quiz attempts, mock test scores, and graded assignments into an estimated exam grade with prediction bands.
- **Confusion Heatmap:** Automatically tracks and surfaces topics where you struggle most.

### 4. Smart Planner & Study Guides
- **Countdown Planner:** Generates structured daily and weekly preparation paths based on your exam dates.
- **Markdown Export:** Export your generated study roadmaps instantly as `.md` files.

---

## Getting Started

1. **Launch the App:** Open `index.html` in your browser or run `npm start` for the desktop app.
2. **Set Your Profile:** Add your subjects, major, and exam goals.
3. **Start Studying:** Use the Timer, Flashcards, or AI Tutor to begin your first study session.
