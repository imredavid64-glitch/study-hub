    🤖 Android

    Install as an app directly from Chrome — no Google Play needed.

    1. Open this site in Chrome on your phone
    2. Tap the ⋮ menu → Install app (or “Add to Home screen”)
    3. Study Hub appears on your home screen like a native app

    📱 iPhone / iPad

    Add Study Hub to your Home Screen from Safari — no App Store needed.

    1. Open this site in Safari on your iPhone or iPad
    2. Tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen
    3. Open it from your Home Screen — it runs full-screen and works offline
